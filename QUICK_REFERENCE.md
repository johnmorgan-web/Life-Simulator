## 🎮 How to Use This Job Board

### For Players
1. **Choose your track**: Pick career path matching your interests
2. **Complete prerequisites**: Work entry jobs to get experience/certs
3. **Progress steadily**: Each level pays more, gets better
4. **Chase elite roles**: Military, executive, and startup founder roles are rare but achievable
5. **Reach the top**: CEO ($20K), CTO ($15K), or billionaire status ($1M!)

### For Developers
1. **Reference careers**: Look at track structure when designing new content
2. **Maintain logic**: Keep prerequisite chains realistic
3. **Financial balance**: Next job always pays more than prerequisite
4. **No nonsense**: Avoid unrelated cert requirements
5. **Clear paths**: 2-3 steps to reach any job
